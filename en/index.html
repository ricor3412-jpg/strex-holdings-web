Who we are

STREX HOLDINGS is an international business platform dedicated to identifying, developing and structuring business opportunities in the energy, oil, petrochemical, mining and infrastructure industries.

Our model combines commercial development, investment management, international procurement and the coordination of specialized capabilities. We work with operators, manufacturers, suppliers and technical companies to shape solutions tailored to the needs of each project.

We bring together international experience and knowledge of local markets, with an emphasis on contractual discipline, operational efficiency, safety and the sustainable creation of value.

Mission

To drive strategic projects and businesses by integrating corporate capabilities, technologies and industrial solutions that strengthen the competitiveness and growth of our clients and markets.

Vision

To become an international business platform recognized for its ability to structure opportunities, coordinate industrial solutions and build long-term strategic relationships.

Strategic development priority

Venezuela: international capabilities for productive recovery and industrial reconstruction

Venezuela is a priority market within STREX HOLDINGS’ business development strategy. Its energy, industrial and logistics potential creates opportunities to rehabilitate productive assets, modernize facilities and restore essential infrastructure.

Our approach is to identify viable projects, bring together international technical capabilities, promote business cooperation schemes and help structure investments that support the recovery of productive activity.

This approach reflects a strategic priority and is not a statement of awarded contracts or committed investments.

Priority lines

  • Recovery of mature and idle wells, drilling, workover and well services.
  • Chemical stimulation, production optimization and surface facilities.
  • Power generation for wells, oilfields and industrial facilities.
  • Rehabilitation of refineries, petrochemical complexes and storage systems.
  • Associated gas recovery, compression, processing and regasification.
  • Marine services, barges, energy logistics and port support.
  • Rehabilitation of water, power, road and industrial infrastructure.
  • Procurement, technology transfer, supplier development and investment structuring.
