Production & Field Optimization

Technologies and services that raise asset productivity, improve the reliability of production facilities and support the recovery of mature fields.

Request a proposal View services
  • Industries Oil & gas exploration and production
  • Focus markets United States and Latin America, with particular focus on Colombia and Venezuela
  • Delivery Direct or through specialized companies, depending on contractual scope

Services and solutions

  • Artificial lift: ESP, PCP, rod pumping and gas lift
  • Multiphase pumping and fluid transfer
  • Early production units and modular facilities
  • Separators, manifolds, flow stations and metering
  • Heavy and extra-heavy crude treatment and handling
  • Well optimization and reactivation of idle wells
  • Produced water handling, treatment and injection
  • Production instrumentation, automation and monitoring
  • Operation and maintenance of surface facilities

Services may be delivered directly or structured through specialized companies, depending on the contractual scope of each operation. Availability is confirmed in each proposal.

Frequently asked questions

Does STREX deliver field production services directly?

The portfolio covers services that may be delivered directly or structured through specialized companies, depending on the contractual scope of each operation. Each proposal defines who performs each component and under which model.

Which markets does STREX Holdings serve?

We focus on developing opportunities in the United States and Latin America, with particular attention to Colombia and Venezuela, and we bring together capabilities from our business ecosystem in international markets.

How can STREX be engaged?

Through supply and procurement, specialized services and equipment rental, operation and maintenance, EPC and EPCM, commercial representation, consortia and joint ventures, or investment structuring. Each scheme is assessed for the specific project.
